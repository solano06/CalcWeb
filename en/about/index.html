About CalcWeb: Open & 100% Client-Side Symbolic Engineering

An academic platform designed for Engineering, Physics, and Mathematics students and educators with step-by-step derivations, pedagogical rigor, and complete privacy.

1. Our Educational Mission (E-E-A-T)

CalcWeb was created to democratize access to university-grade Computer Algebra System (CAS) tools. Unlike traditional calculators that only output a final number or require paid server subscriptions, CalcWeb breaks down every problem step by step, explaining the theorem, trigonometric identity, integrating factor, or elementary row operation applied.

2. How Our Browser-Based Symbolic Engine Works ($0 Server Architecture)

All mathematical intelligence in CalcWeb runs directly on your device's CPU via WebAssembly (Pyodide + SymPy inside a dedicated Web Worker) paired with an instant JavaScript hybrid algebraic solver. This architecture delivers zero latency on standard operations, absolute privacy (none of your equations or matrices are sent to external servers), and offline resilience once loaded.

3. Covered Engineering Disciplines

Our 12 modules cover core undergraduate courses in Electrical, Mechanical, Aerospace, Computer Engineering, and Mathematics: Signals & Systems (Fourier Transform, Laplace Transform, Z-Transform, Convolution, Fourier Series), Calculus & Differential Equations (Step-by-Step Integrals, 1st & 2nd Order ODEs, Taylor Series, Numerical Series), and Linear Algebra & AC Circuits (Matrix Diagonalization, Gauss-Jordan Elimination, and AC Phasors).

4. Academic Contact & Feedback

We continuously review our symbolic algorithms and formula guides for maximum academic accuracy. If you are a university professor or student and wish to suggest a new module or report an issue, contact us at contacto@calcweb.edu.
